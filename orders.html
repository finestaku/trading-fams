Orders fill at the market price when the price is checked, which is at your limit price or better. We check whenever our server is awake. Our free hosting sleeps when nobody is visiting, so an order can fill late, and a very brief price move between checks can be missed. Your cash and coins are checked when you place an order and again when it fills. If you've spent them elsewhere by then, the order is cancelled and we'll tell you why. A 0.5% fee applies to every fill. Virtual trading platform, not financial advice.
